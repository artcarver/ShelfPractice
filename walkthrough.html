Work the item

  • Click a choice, or press A–E, to answer it
  • Right‑click a choice, or click its ab tab, to cross it out
  • Drag across the stem to highlight; click a highlight to lift it
  • Tick Mark for review to flag the item for a second pass
  • Every button in the bar works: Lab Values, Notes, Text size, Review and Pause

Nothing is revealed while you are working, because the block runs the way the shelf does. Ending it reopens every item with the correct answer marked, your own answer beside it, and the form's explanation underneath. The same button sits inside Review, which is where the exam keeps it; there it asks first if you have left items unanswered.

The item itself was written for this page so that the tour gives nothing away. The forms carry the NBME's own stems, choices and answer letters, unedited. Lab Values in the bar above is not a mock-up of the panel either: it is the same component the exam loads, with the same ranges and the same search.

What the frame leaves out

Three things one item on one page cannot act out.

ExhibitsWhere a form prints an image, the item carries it and clicking it opens it full size.
It saves itselfAnswers, highlights, cross‑outs, marks, notes and the clock stay in your browser. Close the tab mid‑block and pick it up later. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.
And it can leaveExport progress at the foot of the exam list writes everything to a file, and Import puts it into another browser, so a block worked on a laptop can be finished on a phone.
