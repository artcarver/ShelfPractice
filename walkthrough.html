These are not screenshots. Every form runs as a question interface, and what follows is that screen with one live item in it, built out of the exam page's own stylesheet rather than pictured. Everything in the frame responds.

  • Right‑click a choice, or click its ab tab, to cross it out
  • Drag across the stem to highlight; click a highlight to lift it
  • Tick Mark for review to flag the item for a second pass

Nothing is revealed while you are working, because the block runs the way the shelf does. Ending it reopens every item with the correct answer marked, your own answer beside it, and the form's explanation underneath. In a real exam the button lives behind Review → End block and see results, and it asks first if you have left items unanswered.

The item itself was written for this page so that the tour gives nothing away. The forms carry the NBME's own stems, choices and answer letters, unedited.

3

The rest of the bar

One item cannot act all of this out, so here it is in words.

Lab ValuesThe reference ranges, searchable and tabbed, in a panel beside the question. The same table the real exam gives you.
NotesA small window you drag where you like and leave open. One note per item, saved as you type, and shown again on the score sheet.
PauseStops the clock and hides the question. Leave the page paused and it is still paused when you come back.
Text sizeFour steps, applied to the stem, the choices and the explanation. The chrome keeps its own size.
ExhibitsWhere a form prints an image, the item carries it and clicking it opens it full size.
It saves itselfAnswers, highlights, cross‑outs, marks, notes and the clock stay in your browser. Close the tab mid‑block and pick it up later. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.
